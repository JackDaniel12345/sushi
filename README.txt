HUSH SUSHI & CO. — STANDALONE FIVE-PAGE WEBSITE

GETTING STARTED
1. Extract this entire ZIP into one folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open the home page in a modern browser.
3. Keep the assets folder, styles.css, and script.js beside the HTML pages.
No React, dependencies, installation, server, or build step is required.
For hosting, upload the folder contents to any static web host.

PAGES
index.html — Home
menu.html — Menu with category filters
about.html — Restaurant story
reservations.html — Demonstration booking form
contact.html — Demonstration inquiry form

IMPORTANT SAMPLE CONTENT
Hush Sushi & Co. is a fictional restaurant concept inspired by the supplied
Pinterest reference https://pin.it/6gfdwbl9K, not a copy of a real business.
The story, dishes, prices (Singapore dollars), 12 Sakura Lane address,
hello@hush.example email, and Tuesday–Sunday 12:00–22:00 opening hours
are invented sample content and must be replaced before business use.
All photographs are AI-generated and illustrate the concept, not real dishes.
Dish names and allergen notes must be verified against the real kitchen.

FORMS
Both forms validate locally and display sample confirmations only.
They do not send, save, email, or confirm anything. No payment or booking
system is included. Connect a real form/booking service and update the
privacy disclosures and confirmation wording before accepting customers.
Dates cannot be in the past. Availability, opening days, and capacity are
not checked. A future production booking service must enforce those rules.

CUSTOMIZATION
Edit the HTML files to replace copy, hours, prices, and contact information.
Shared colours and spacing are defined in styles.css using CSS variables.
Menu filtering, mobile navigation, and demo forms live in script.js.
Images and the brand favicon are local in assets/ and work offline.
There are no external fonts, libraries, trackers, or network dependencies.

ACCESSIBILITY
Semantic sections, page-specific titles and descriptions, visible focus
states, skip links, labelled forms, keyboard-accessible navigation, live
feedback, image descriptions, and reduced-motion handling are included.
