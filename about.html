A little about us

Our story

A love of Japanese craft, a taste for something new, and a table for everyone.

Welcome to Hush

Tradition, with room
for curiosity.

Hush began with a simple idea: take the care and precision of Japanese sushi, and bring it to a relaxed, modern table.

Our menu celebrates the familiar — delicate nigiri, clean sashimi, perfectly seasoned rice — while making space for playful textures and unexpected combinations.

Whether you join us for a quiet lunch or a long dinner with friends, we want every bite to feel considered, and every visit to feel easy.
